Sound / equalizer

Changes apply instantly — adjust while ChatGPT is talking. Bass cut removes lows a small speaker cannot play (more clean loudness); warmth makes it fuller; less treble makes it less tinny.

Volume sets everything; voice boost lifts only ChatGPT's voice (speech is much quieter on average than the chimes). Loud peaks are softened instead of clipped. The test sound is a steady 2 s tone: if it crackles, check the speaker wire.

Say “Seneca” (SEN-e-ka) to start a conversation. Say “Seneca, stop” to end it. Speak over a reply to interrupt. Connect headphones or a speaker to hear replies.

AirPlay output (experimental)

Send sound to an AirPlay speaker such as a HomePod mini. Step 1: find speakers, pick one, send a short test tone (2 s, 440 Hz, about 2 s delay).

Calls use the chosen AirPlay speaker automatically (it connects while the call starts; ChatGPT sounds ~2 s later). The trim shifts the echo-cancellation reference if Seneca starts hearing itself.

Sign in to ChatGPT

Import your current ChatGPT session. The speaker keeps it in device storage and never displays it again.

  1. Sign in at chatgpt.com in Safari.
  2. Open Develop → Show Web Inspector → Storage → Cookies → chatgpt.com.
  3. Copy the value of __Secure-next-auth.session-token.0 into the first field and .1 into the second. For a single unnumbered cookie, use only the first field.
